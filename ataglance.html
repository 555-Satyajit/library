About Us

At a Glance

The establishment of the Harekrushna Mahtab State Library traces back to the First Five-Year Plan, when the Government of India encouraged the development of state-level library infrastructure. The present building, completed in 1959, stands on nearly three acres of land in the capital city of Bhubaneswar . In 1967, the premises were designated as Gandhi Bhawan in tribute to the Father of the Nation, Mahatma Gandhi. A significant phase of growth began in 1986 when Prof. D. R. Kalia, an eminent authority in public library services, was entrusted with guiding its renovation and modernization. The renovation, completed in 1987, enabled the building to accommodate two libraries: the State Library, serving as the State Central Library, and the Bhubaneswar Public Library, functioning as the apex library of the city’s public library system. In the same year, the institution was officially renamed in honor of Harekrushna Mahatab, a prominent architect of modern Odisha. At present, the State Library serves as a reference and research Centre. The building also accommodates the Bhubaneswar Public Library, which functions as the city’s central library and provides lending and reading room facilities to the public.

Detailed information about the State Library and Public Library

Harekrushna Mahtab State Library

The Harekrushna Mahtab State Library functions as a premier reference and research institution catering to scholars, researchers, and the general reading public of Odisha.

The state library has been divided into three main divisions:

  • Administrative Services Division (ASD)
  • Technical Service Division (TSD)
  • Reader Services Division (RSD)

The Administrative Service Division oversees matters related to general administration, establishment, finance, accounts, and maintenance, ensuring smooth and efficient functioning of the institution.

The Technical Service Division is responsible for the core technical operations of the library, including the selection, acquisition, classification, cataloguing, conservation, and binding of library materials. In addition to regular purchases, the library receives publications under the provisions of the Press and Registration of Books Act, 1867, and from the Raja Ram Mohan Roy Library Foundation, Kolkata.

The Reader Service Division manages activities related to the registration of members, organization of documents, reference services, circulation of books and periodicals, and reprography services.

Reference Collection: All reference materials published on various subjects, including national and international publications, are organized by subject according to the DDC system. The collection includes dictionaries, handbooks, encyclopedias, bibliographies, etc.

Area Study Collection: The library adopts a specialized classification schedule for the area studies collection devised by Prof. D. R. Kalia. To support in-depth research, the section organizes documents into specific area collections:

  • Odisha Collection: Devoted to works on Odisha, covering political, economic, educational, historical, and cultural dimensions, irrespective of place of publication.
  • National Collection: Deals with books related to India, with collections of different states arranged to facilitate comparative study with Odisha.
  • International Collection: Comprises books concerning the world at large, arranged to support comparative study with Odisha.
  • Tribal Collection: Developed to support research on socio-economic and cultural aspects of tribal communities, with emphasis on Odisha tribes.
  • Fine Arts Collection: Includes monographs and multi-volume sets from national and international agencies, covering architecture, sculpture, folk arts, sports, music, etc.
  • Women's Studies Collection: Comprises documents related to research on women's issues and contributions across disciplines.
  • Biography Collection: Divided into National Biography, International Biography, and Odisha Biography. For user convenience, it is further subdivided into BA (Autobiography), BI (Individual Biography), and BC (Collective Biography).

The library organizes official documents to meet the specific information needs of researchers and users. The collection includes International Official Documents, National Official Documents, and Odisha Official Documents. It contains administrative reports of government departments, annual reports, census reports, statistical reports, budgets, survey reports, gazetteers, legal documents, gazette notifications, and legislative documents.

The library has established a Corporate Publication Collection, encompassing documents from corporate bodies, the United Nations and allied agencies, government-sponsored autonomous agencies, UGC, and research institutes.

The depository section is designed to deal with books in Odisha. In accordance with the Press and Registration of Books Act, 1867, as implemented by the state government, the library regularly receives two legal deposit copies of every book from the printer, publisher, or author. The Department of Culture also requires deposit receipts for consideration under the RRLF scheme.

Of the two copies received:

  • The first copy is preserved in the depository section for preservation and compilation of Odisha State Bibliography.
  • The second copy is transferred to the respective section of the Reader Services Division for public access and use.

The Language and Literature Section contains books in Odia, English, Hindi, Sanskrit, Bengali, and Urdu covering both language and literary studies. Alongside Odia, the section includes works in other Indian languages as well as select foreign literatures, creating a diverse and inclusive literary collection. The section has grown progressively over the years and now possesses a rich collection, particularly in Odia studies. At present, this section provides services to users of both the HKM State Library and the Bhubaneswar Public Library.

Bhubaneswar Public Library

The Bhubaneswar Public Library provides lending and reading facilities to the general public, students, and children.

The library has established a dedicated children section in accordance with the library rules. This section is especially designed to cater to children in the age group of 5 to 14 years. Old Odia children magazines are bound and preserved for reference purposes. The section offers a rich and diverse collection for young minds and has adequate information sources to help students complete their school assignments.

The textbook and career development section, a part of Bhubaneswar Public Library, is dedicated to the collection and organization of curriculum-oriented materials for students pursuing higher education. It maintains a wide range of career development resources for young readers preparing for competitive exams and entrance tests. Being one of the most dynamic sections of the library, it accounts for nearly half of the total user engagement.

The subject collection section organizes theoretical aspects on all subjects except textbooks and career development materials. It caters to readers with general academic interest and serves supplementary reading support for undergraduate and master's degree students.

The serials section is a part of Bhubaneswar Public Library and provides newspaper and periodical services to readers. The library compiles indexes to Odia newspapers covering different aspects of Odisha. It also prepares indexes to Odia periodicals and preserves back volumes for future reference.

The library has established a braille section to serve visually impaired readers and promote inclusive library services. In addition, braille magazines are also received as gifts from USA.
